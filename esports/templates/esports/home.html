How It Works

From raw player stats to an explainable, leakage-safe prediction in four steps.

01

Rolling History

For each match, we compute every player's career average stats from all prior matches, with zero leakage from the game being predicted.

02

Team Features

Player stats (ACS, KD, KAST, ADR, FK, FD, HS%, Rating) are aggregated per team — mean, peak, consistency — then differenced (Team A − Team B).

03

Ensemble Prediction

Three models vote: Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and XGBoost. The average of their probabilities is the final prediction.

04

Time-Based Split

Trained on 2021–2023, validated on 2024, tested on 2025–2026, so future information never leaks into training.
