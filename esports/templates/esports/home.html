How It Works

01

Rolling History

For each match, we compute every player's career average stats from all prior matches — zero leakage from the game being predicted.

02

Team Features

Player stats (ACS, KD, KAST, ADR, FK, FD, HS%, Rating) are aggregated per team — mean, peak, and consistency — then differenced (Team A − Team B).

03

Ensemble Prediction

Three models vote: Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and XGBoost. The ensemble average probability is the final prediction.

04

Time-Based Split

Trained on 2021–2023, validated on 2024, tested on 2025–2026. Prevents future information leaking into training.
